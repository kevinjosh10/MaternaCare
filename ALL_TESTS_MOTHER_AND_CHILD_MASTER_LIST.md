### 1.1 First Trimester Tests (Weeks 1 – 13)

| # | Test Name | Timing | Specimen / Modality | Clinical Purpose & What It Detects |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Urine Pregnancy Test (hCG)** | Weeks 1 – 4 | Urine | Qualitative confirmation of human chorionic gonadotropin. |
| **2** | **Quantitative Serum $\beta$-hCG** | Weeks 1 – 6 | Venous Blood | Quantitative pregnancy confirmation; serial 48-hr doubling check to rule out ectopic pregnancy or early miscarriage. |
| **3** | **Dating & Viability Ultrasound (TVS)** | Weeks 6 – 8 | Transvaginal Ultrasound | Confirms intrauterine pregnancy, gestational sac, yolk sac, Crown-Rump Length (CRL), and fetal heart rate (FHR). |
| **4** | **Complete Blood Count (CBC)** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Whole Blood (EDTA) | Baseline Hemoglobin ($\text{Hb} \ge 11.0\text{ g/dL}$), Hematocrit, Platelets, White Blood Cell count. |
| **5** | **Blood Grouping (ABO) & Rh(D) Factor** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Whole Blood | Identifies maternal blood group and Rh-negative status to prevent Rh isoimmunization. |
| **6** | **Indirect Coombs Test (ICT / Antibody Screen)** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Serum | Detects circulating maternal atypical red cell antibodies (anti-D, Kell, Duffy). |
| **7** | **HIV 1 & 2 ELISA / CLIA Screen** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Serum | Universal screening for HIV to initiate antiretroviral therapy and prevent vertical transmission. |
| **8** | **Hepatitis B Surface Antigen (HBsAg)** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Serum | Identifies HBV infection to provide neonatal immunoprophylaxis (HBIg + vaccine at birth). |
| **9** | **Hepatitis C Antibody (Anti-HCV)** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Serum | Screens for chronic Hepatitis C viremia. |
| **10** | **Syphilis Serology (VDRL / RPR / TPHA)** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Serum | Screens for Treponema pallidum to prevent congenital syphilis and stillbirth. |
| **11** | **Rubella IgG Antibody Titer** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Serum | Confirms immunity ($>10\text{ IU/mL}$) against congenital Rubella syndrome. |
| **12** | **Varicella Zoster IgG** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Serum | Assesses immunity against chickenpox in non-immunized mothers. |
| **13** | **Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH)** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Serum | First trimester thyroid screening (target $< 2.5\text{ mIU/L}$) to protect fetal neurological development. |
| **14** | **Free Thyroxine (Free T4)** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Serum | Evaluates overt vs. subclinical maternal hypothyroidism/hyperthyroidism. |
| **15** | **Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Plasma | Baseline glucose to detect pre-existing overt diabetes mellitus ($< 92\text{ mg/dL}$). |
| **16** | **Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c)** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Whole Blood | Assesses 3-month glycemic control (target $< 5.7\%$). |
| **17** | **Urine Routine & Microscopic Exam (R/M)** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Midstream Urine | Screens for proteinuria, hematuria, glucosuria, and pyuria. |
| **18** | **Urine Culture & Sensitivity (ASB Screen)** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Clean-Catch Urine | Screens for Asymptomatic Bacteriuria ($\ge 10^5\text{ CFU/mL}$) to prevent pyelonephritis and preterm labor. |
| **19** | **Hemoglobin HPLC / Hb Electrophoresis** | Weeks 8 – 10 | Whole Blood | Screens for Thalassemia minor ($HbA_2 > 3.5\%$) and Sickle Cell traits. |
| **20** | **Cell-Free DNA (cfDNA / NIPT)** | Weeks 10 – 13+ | Maternal Blood | Non-invasive genetic screen for Trisomy 21 (Down), Trisomy 18, Trisomy 13, sex chromosome aneuploidies, and fetal sex. |
| **21** | **Nuchal Translucency (NT) Scan** | Weeks 11 – 13+6 | Ultrasound | Measures fetal nuchal subcutaneous fluid ($< 2.5\text{ mm}$ normal); checks presence of nasal bone. |
| **22** | **Serum PAPP-A** | Weeks 11 – 13+6 | Serum | Pregnancy-Associated Plasma Protein-A (decreased in Trisomies and placental insufficiency). |
| **23** | **Serum Free $\beta$-hCG (First Trimester)** | Weeks 11 – 13+6 | Serum | First trimester combined screen biochemical marker. |
| **24** | **Uterine Artery Doppler (T1 Preeclampsia Screen)** | Weeks 11 – 13+6 | Color Doppler USG | Measures Mean Uterine Artery Pulsatility Index (UtA-PI) to calculate preeclampsia risk. |
| **25** | **Serum Placental Growth Factor (PlGF)** | Weeks 11 – 13+6 | Serum | Early biomarker for preeclampsia and placental function. |
| **26** | **Chorionic Villus Sampling (CVS)** *(Indicated)* | Weeks 10 – 13+6 | Placental Biopsy | Invasive diagnostic cytogenetics (karyotyping & chromosomal microarray). |

### 1.2 Second Trimester Tests (Weeks 14 – 27)

| # | Test Name | Timing | Specimen / Modality | Clinical Purpose & What It Detects |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **27** | **Maternal Serum Alpha-Fetoprotein (MSAFP)** | Weeks 15 – 20 | Serum | Screens for Open Neural Tube Defects (Spina Bifida, Anencephaly) and abdominal wall defects. |
| **28** | **Second Trimester Total hCG** | Weeks 15 – 20 | Serum | Component of Quadruple Screen for chromosomal abnormalities. |
| **29** | **Unconjugated Estriol ($uE_3$)** | Weeks 15 – 20 | Serum | Component of Quadruple Screen (low in Down and Edwards syndromes). |
| **30** | **Dimeric Inhibin-A (DIA)** | Weeks 15 – 20 | Serum | Component of Quadruple Screen (elevated in Down syndrome). |
| **31** | **Diagnostic Amniocentesis** *(Indicated)* | Weeks 15 – 22 | Amniotic Fluid | Definitive fetal karyotyping, QF-PCR, microarray, and viral PCR (CMV/Toxoplasma). |
| **32** | **Level-II Target / TIFFA Anatomy Scan** | Weeks 18 – 22 | Comprehensive USG | Evaluates full fetal structural anatomy: brain ventricles, spine, 4-chamber heart, kidneys, stomach, face/lips, limbs, cord insertion. |
| **33** | **Transvaginal Cervical Length Measurement** | Weeks 18 – 22 | Transvaginal USG | Measures cervical length ($> 25\text{ mm}$ normal); detects cervical incompetence for Cerclage/Progesterone. |
| **34** | **Fetal Echocardiography** *(Indicated)* | Weeks 20 – 24 | Specialized Fetal Echo | Detailed structural and hemodynamic assessment of fetal heart chambers, valves, and great vessels. |
| **35** | **75g 2-Hour Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT)** | Weeks 24 – 28 | Venous Plasma | Universal screen for Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (Fasting, 1-hr, and 2-hr plasma glucose). |
| **36** | **Repeat Complete Blood Count (CBC - T2)** | Weeks 24 – 28 | Whole Blood | Checks for second-trimester physiological gestational anemia ($\text{Hb} \ge 10.5\text{ g/dL}$). |
| **37** | **Repeat Indirect Coombs Test (ICT - T2)** | Weeks 24 – 28 | Serum | Antibody screen for Rh-negative mothers before receiving 28-week Anti-D immunoglobulin. |

### 1.3 Third Trimester Tests (Weeks 28 – 40+)

| # | Test Name | Timing | Specimen / Modality | Clinical Purpose & What It Detects |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **38** | **Third Trimester Growth Ultrasound** | Weeks 28 – 32 | Ultrasound Biometry | Calculates Estimated Fetal Weight (Hadlock EFW), BPD, HC, AC, FL; detects FGR and Macrosomia. |
| **39** | **Amniotic Fluid Index (AFI) / SDP** | Weeks 28 – 40 | Ultrasound | Quantifies amniotic fluid volume (AFI $8.0 – 24.0\text{ cm}$; SDP $2.0 – 8.0\text{ cm}$); screens Oligo/Polyhydramnios. |
| **40** | **Umbilical Artery (UA) Doppler** | Weeks 28 – 40 | Color Doppler USG | Assesses placental resistance (Pulsatility Index, S/D ratio, absent/reversed end-diastolic velocity). |
| **41** | **Middle Cerebral Artery (MCA) Doppler** | Weeks 28 – 40 | Color Doppler USG | Peak Systolic Velocity (PSV) screens for fetal anemia; Pulsatility Index screens for brain-sparing hypoxia. |
| **42** | **Ductus Venosus (DV) Doppler** | Weeks 28 – 40 | Color Doppler USG | Assesses right ventricular cardiac function and acidemia in growth-restricted fetuses. |
| **43** | **Cerebroplacental Ratio (CPR)** | Weeks 28 – 40 | Doppler Calculation | MCA-PI / UA-PI ratio; early marker of fetal compromise. |
| **44** | **Serial Antenatal Blood Pressure Monitoring** | Every Visit | Sphygmomanometer | Screens for Gestational Hypertension and Preeclampsia ($\ge 140/90\text{ mmHg}$). |
| **45** | **Urine Protein Dipstick / Spot UPCR** | Every Visit | Urine | Screens for proteinuria ($\text{UPCR} \ge 0.3\text{ mg/mg}$ indicates preeclampsia). |
| **46** | **Serum sFlt-1 / PlGF Biomarker Ratio** | Weeks 28 – 37 | Serum | Predicts short-term onset and ruling out of preeclampsia ($\text{ratio} \le 38$ rules out). |
| **47** | **Liver Function Tests (AST / ALT / Bilirubin)** | Third Trimester | Serum | Screens for HELLP syndrome and liver dysfunction in preeclampsia. |
| **48** | **Renal Function Tests (Creatinine / Uric Acid)** | Third Trimester | Serum | Screens for renal impairment in hypertensive disorders of pregnancy. |
| **49** | **Serum Lactate Dehydrogenase (LDH)** | Third Trimester | Serum | Diagnostic marker for microangiopathic hemolysis in HELLP syndrome ($>600\text{ IU/L}$). |
| **50** | **Fasting Total Serum Bile Acids (TBA)** | Third Trimester | Serum | Diagnoses Intrahepatic Cholestasis of Pregnancy (ICP) (severe if $\ge 40 – 100\ \mu\text{mol/L}$). |
| **51** | **Group B Streptococcus (GBS) Swab** | Weeks 35 – 37 | Vagino-Rectal Swab | Cultures for GBS colonization to mandate intrapartum IV Ampicillin/Penicillin prophylaxis. |
| **52** | **Coagulation Profile (PT / INR / aPTT / Fibrinogen)** | Weeks 36 – 38 | Plasma | Pre-delivery baseline to rule out coagulopathy and prepare for anesthesia. |
| **53** | **Fetal Presentation Ultrasound** | Weeks 36 – 38 | Ultrasound | Confirms fetal lie (Cephalic vs. Breech vs. Transverse) and placental clearance of internal os. |
| **54** | **Cardiotocography (CTG) / Non-Stress Test (NST)** | Weeks 36 – 40+ | Electronic Fetal Monitor | Evaluates 20-min fetal heart rate baseline (110–160 bpm), variability, and accelerations ($15\times 15$). |
| **55** | **Manning's Biophysical Profile (BPP)** | Weeks 36 – 40+ | USG + CTG | 5-variable fetal well-being score (NST, Breathing, Movement, Tone, Amniotic Fluid) out of 10. |
| **56** | **Modified Biophysical Profile (mBPP)** | Weeks 36 – 40+ | CTG + USG | Reactive NST combined with Single Deepest Pocket (SDP $\ge 2\text{ cm}$). |
| **57** | **Cervical Bishop Score** | Weeks 38 – 41+ | Pelvic Examination | Measures cervical dilation, effacement, station, consistency, and position for labor induction readiness. |
| **58** | **Daily Fetal Kick Count Assessment** | Daily (W28+) | Maternal Perception | Monitors fetal neuro-motor vigor (target $\ge 10$ distinct kicks in 2 hours). |

### 1.5 Postpartum Maternal Tests (Day 1 to 1 Year)

| # | Test Name | Timing | Specimen / Modality | Clinical Purpose & What It Detects |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **63** | **Post-Delivery Complete Blood Count (CBC)** | Day 1 – 2 | Whole Blood | Quantifies postpartum blood loss and detects acute postpartum anemia ($\text{Hb} < 10.0\text{ g/dL}$). |
| **64** | **Cord Blood ABO / Rh Typing** | Day 1 (At Birth) | Fetal Cord Blood | Determines newborn Rh status to verify if maternal Anti-D immunoglobulin is required. |
| **65** | **Kleihauer-Betke (KB) Acid Elution Test / Rosette Screen** | Day 1 – 2 | Maternal Blood | Quantifies volume of feto-maternal hemorrhage to calculate precise Anti-D Rh immunoglobulin dose. |
| **66** | **Postpartum Uterine Tone & Lochia Assessment** | Day 1 – 14 | Physical Exam | Detects uterine atony, subinvolution, and secondary postpartum hemorrhage. |
| **67** | **Early Postpartum Blood Pressure Profile** | Days 3 – 7 | Sphygmomanometer | Screens for late-onset/delayed postpartum preeclampsia in high-risk mothers. |
| **68** | **Surgical Incision & Perineal Healing Exam (REEDA)** | Week 1 – 2 | Physical Inspection | Inspects C-section Pfannenstiel scar or perineal laceration for dehiscence, hematoma, or infection. |
| **69** | **Postpartum 75g 2-Hour Oral Glucose Tolerance Test** | Week 6 | Venous Plasma | Mandatory for all GDM mothers to reclassify metabolic status (Normal vs. Prediabetes vs. Type 2 Diabetes). |
| **70** | **Postpartum Thyroid Function Panel (TSH & Free T4)** | Week 6 – Month 6 | Serum | Screens for Postpartum Thyroiditis (biphasic thyrotoxicosis followed by hypothyroidism). |
| **71** | **Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS / PHQ-9)** | Week 6 & Months 3, 6, 12 | Validated Survey | Universal screening for maternal postpartum depression, perinatal anxiety, and self-harm risk. |
| **72** | **Cervical Cytology (Pap Smear / Liquid-Based)** | Week 6+ | Cervical Scrape | Resumes cervical cancer screening if due. |
| **73** | **Postnatal Pelvic Floor & Musculoskeletal Assessment** | Week 6 | Physical Exam | Evaluates diastasis recti, pelvic organ prolapse, and urinary incontinence. |
| **74** | **Maternal Micronutrient Panel (Ferritin, Vit D, B12)** | Month 3 – 6 | Serum | Evaluates nutritional depletion in breastfeeding mothers. |
| **75** | **Annual Cardiometabolic Profile (Lipids + FBS + HbA1c)** | Month 12 | Serum / Plasma | Long-term cardiovascular risk stratification for mothers with past preeclampsia, GDM, or preterm birth. |

### 2.1 Immediate Newborn Tests (0 – 72 Hours)

| # | Test Name | Timing | Specimen / Modality | Clinical Purpose & What It Detects |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **76** | **1-Minute Apgar Scoring** | 1 Min Post-Birth | Clinical Assessment | Evaluates immediate neonatal cardiopulmonary and neuromuscular transition. |
| **77** | **5-Minute Apgar Scoring** | 5 Min Post-Birth | Clinical Assessment | Gold-standard index of resuscitation response and neonatal vitality ($7-10$ normal). |
| **78** | **10-Minute Apgar Scoring** *(Indicated)* | 10 Min Post-Birth | Clinical Assessment | Performed if 5-minute score is $< 7$ to track ongoing resuscitation trajectory. |
| **79** | **Umbilical Cord Blood Gas Analysis (pH & Base Deficit)** | Immediate Birth | Cord Arterial Blood | Validates presence or absence of intrapartum asphyxia and metabolic acidosis ($\text{pH} < 7.00$, $\text{BD} \ge 12$). |
| **80** | **Critical Congenital Heart Disease (CCHD) Pulse Oximetry** | $\ge 24$ Hours | Pulse Oximetry | Compares pre-ductal (right hand) vs. post-ductal (foot) $SpO_2$ to detect duct-dependent cyanotic heart lesions. |
| **81** | **Automated Auditory Brainstem Response (AABR)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Electrophysiologic | Universal Newborn Hearing Screen measuring neural transmission from cochlea to brainstem. |
| **82** | **Otoacoustic Emissions (OAE / DPOAE)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Acoustic Echo Probe | Universal Newborn Hearing Screen measuring hair cell function within the cochlea. |
| **83** | **Transcutaneous Bilirubin (TcB)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Optical Skin Sensor | Non-invasive optical forehead/sternal jaundice screening. |
| **84** | **Total Serum Bilirubin (TSB) & Direct Bilirubin** | 24 – 72 Hours | Venous / Capillary Blood | Plotted on Bhutani Nomogram to assess need for phototherapy / exchange transfusion; prevents Kernicterus. |
| **85** | **Point-of-Care Neonatal Blood Glucose** | 1 – 24 Hours | Capillary Blood | Screens for neonatal hypoglycemia ($< 40-45\text{ mg/dL}$) in infants of diabetic mothers, SGA, or LGA. |

### 2.2 Inborn Errors of Metabolism & Genetic Blood Spot Screen (Guthrie Panel)
*Collected between **24 and 48 hours of life** via heel-prick capillary blood on filter paper (Whatman 903):*

| # | Test Name / Disease Target | Timing | Primary Biomarker / Technology | Clinical Danger Prevented by Early Diagnosis |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **86** | **Phenylketonuria (PKU)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated Phenylalanine, Phe/Tyr ratio (MS/MS) | Irreversible severe intellectual disability, microcephaly, seizures. |
| **87** | **Maple Syrup Urine Disease (MSUD)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine | Acute ketoacidosis, cerebral edema, neonatal death. |
| **88** | **Homocystinuria** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated Methionine & Total Homocysteine | Ectopia lentis, thromboembolism, intellectual delay. |
| **89** | **Classic Galactosemia** | 24 – 48 Hours | GALT enzyme activity & Total Galactose | Neonatal E. coli sepsis, hepatic failure, cataracts, death. |
| **90** | **Congenital Hypothyroidism (CH)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated primary TSH / Low Free $T_4$ | Cretinism, growth failure, permanent cognitive impairment. |
| **91** | **Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia (CAH)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated $17\text{-}\alpha\text{-hydroxyprogesterone}$ ($17\text{-OHP}$) | Fatal salt-wasting shock, adrenal crisis, ambiguous genitalia. |
| **92** | **Medium-Chain Acyl-CoA Dehydrogenase (MCAD) Deficiency** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated Octanoylcarnitine ($C_8$) | Sudden unexplained infant death (SIDS-like), hypoketotic hypoglycemia. |
| **93** | **Very Long-Chain Acyl-CoA Dehydrogenase (VLCAD) Deficiency** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated Tetradecenoylcarnitine ($C_{14:1}$) | Cardiomyopathy, hepatic encephalopathy, cardiac arrest. |
| **94** | **Carnitine Palmitoyltransferase (CPT-I & II) Deficiency** | 24 – 48 Hours | Free Carnitine ($C_0$) & Long-Chain Acylcarnitines | Hypoglycemia, rhabdomyolysis, cardiac arrhythmias. |
| **95** | **Propionic Acidemia (PA)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated Propionylcarnitine ($C_3$) | Severe metabolic ketoacidosis, hyperammonemic coma. |
| **96** | **Methylmalonic Acidemia (MMA)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated Propionylcarnitine ($C_3$) & Methylmalonic Acid | Metabolic decompensation, renal failure, lethargy. |
| **97** | **Isovaleric Acidemia (IVA)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated Isovalerylcarnitine ($C_5$) | "Sweaty feet" odor, vomiting, metabolic acidosis, coma. |
| **98** | **Glutaric Acidemia Type 1 (GA-1)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated Glutarylcarnitine ($C_5\text{DC}$) | Acute encephalopathic crisis with bilateral striatal necrosis. |
| **99** | **Cystic Fibrosis (CF)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Elevated Immunoreactive Trypsinogen (IRT) + CFTR DNA | Chronic bronchopulmonary disease, pancreatic insufficiency, failure to thrive. |
| **100** | **Severe Combined Immunodeficiency (SCID)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Low T-cell Receptor Excision Circles (TREC) | Fatal opportunistic infections ("bubble boy" disease). |
| **101** | **Spinal Muscular Atrophy (SMA)** | 24 – 48 Hours | Real-Time PCR Exon 7 deletion in *SMN1* gene | Degeneration of spinal lower motor neurons, respiratory paralysis. |
| **102** | **Sickle Cell Anemia & Hemoglobinopathies** | 24 – 48 Hours | Isoelectric Focusing / HPLC ($HbSS, HbSC, HbS\beta\text{-thal}$) | Splenic sequestration, pneumococcal sepsis, vaso-occlusive crises. |
| **103** | **Biotinidase Deficiency** | 24 – 48 Hours | Biotinidase colorimetric enzyme assay | Seizures, hypotonia, ataxia, alopecia, developmental delay. |
| **104** | **Glucose-6-Phosphate Dehydrogenase (G6PD) Deficiency** | 24 – 48 Hours | Quantitative G6PD enzymatic assay | Acute severe hemolytic crisis, hyperbilirubinemia, Kernicterus. |

### 2.3 Early Infancy Tests (Weeks 1 – 4)

| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **106** | **Umbilical Cord Stump & Granuloma Evaluation** | Week 1 – 2 | Physical Inspection | Checks separation of cord, omphalitis (infection), patent urachus, or umbilical granuloma. |

| **109** | **Developmental Hip Stability (Barlow & Ortolani Tests)** | Week 2 – Month 1 | Physical Maneuvers | Detects Developmental Dysplasia of the Hip (DDH) - Barlow detects dislocatability; Ortolani detects reducibility. |
